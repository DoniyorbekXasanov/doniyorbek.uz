A Personal Note

Where It All Started

There was a time when I didn't know what a variable was. I had no idea how a loop worked, and the thought of solving problems with code felt impossible. Programming wasn't just difficult — it was a complete unknown.

Then I walked into your class.

You didn't rush. You didn't assume I already knew things. You started from the very beginning, patiently explaining concepts that probably seemed trivial to you but felt like mountains to me. You showed me that programming isn't about memorizing syntax — it's about learning how to think clearly, break down problems, and build solutions piece by piece.

You gave me something more valuable than any textbook could: the confidence to try, to make mistakes, and to keep going. Because of your teaching, programming went from something I had to learn to something I genuinely enjoy.

And now, as you help me prepare for ICPC, the journey that started in your classroom continues. I'm grateful that the person who introduced me to programming is also the one helping me take the next step.

This page is a small way of saying thank you.
